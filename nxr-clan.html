Esports Team

NXR — No X Retreat

A competitive clan built on forward momentum, disciplined mechanics, and sportsmanship in Roblox Rivals.

The Clan Ethos: "No X Retreat"

Founded as a banner for ambitious players, NXR stands for No X Retreat. The name represents our core in-game doctrine: never backing down from high-skill matchups, embracing tough mechanical duels, and treating every defeat as research for future victories.

Competitive Focus Areas

Discipline & Composure

Prioritizing mental focus during tight rounds and avoiding reactionary mistakes.

Close-Quarters Mastery

Special training sessions devoted to melee, dash mechanics, and tight angle clearing.

Joining & Scrimmage Requests

Clan NXR welcomes scrimmages with other competitive squads. While recruitment is selective, passionate Bangladeshi players who exhibit top-tier movement, respectful demeanor, and dedication to Roblox Rivals are encouraged to connect.

To arrange clan scrimmages or tryouts, submit your Roblox username and recent match VOD through the official contact page.
